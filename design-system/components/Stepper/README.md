# Stepper

The stepper shows where someone is in a Start online form: service and step count, a progress bar, and the step names with completed ones ticked.

- Step 1 is always "Start" and saves the lead; say so in the step intro.
- Completed steps show ✓ in `success`; the current step is bold `blue`; later steps are muted.
- Put the time estimate on the right ("About 8 minutes in total").
- Pair with a *Finish later, just contact me* ghost button on every step after the first.
