# Card

A card is one linked item (a service, programme, opening, post or resource) with a title, a short line and a clear next action.

- **Light** (white, `line` border) on `blue-tint` sections; **Tint** on white sections; **Blue** for the one card that leads to the next stage.
- Eyebrow = category in caps (`MALTA · BSC`); title in `h4`; body in `body-sm` muted; action last, in blue, ending with → or the WhatsApp keyword.
- The whole card is the link. Keep body text to one or two lines; tuition is "paid to the school" and dated on the page.
- Never show a school name on a public card.
