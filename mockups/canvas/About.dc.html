Our story

It started at the KNUST gate.

ErnestLinks Consult began as a simple idea: make global education and travel reachable for everyone. We started by helping KNUST students through passport and visa processes.

Today we run passport drives with KNUST colleges, guide students through school and visa applications, prepare people for proficiency exams, and plan travel. We work from two offices in Kumasi and answer questions live on TikTok every weekday.

Why we exist

PURPOSETo break down the barriers to international education and travel, so people can reach their full potential.
VISIONTo be the leading consultancy in international education and travel, known for transforming lives through global learning.
MISSIONTo guide people through the complexities of education and travel abroad with expert advice and personal support.
